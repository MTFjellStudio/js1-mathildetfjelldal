Privacy Statement

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This statement explains what personal data Rainy Days collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

On this page

  1. Information we collect
  2. How we use your information
  3. Sharing your information
  4. Cookies and local storage
  5. How long we keep data
  6. Your rights
  7. Changes to this statement
  8. Contact us

1. Information we collect

We collect only the information we need to run our shop:

  • Contact details, such as your name, email address and phone number.
  • Delivery and billing addresses when you place an order.
  • Order history and the items you add to your cart.
  • Technical data, such as browser type and pages visited.

2. How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To answer your questions and provide customer support.
  • To send newsletters, only if you have subscribed.
  • To improve our website, products and services.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

3. Sharing your information

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted partners who help us operate, such as payment providers and delivery carriers, and only as much as they need to do their job. We may also disclose data if required by law.

4. Cookies and local storage

Our website stores your shopping cart in your browser's local storage so it is still there when you return. We may also use cookies to understand how the site is used. You can clear local storage or block cookies in your browser settings at any time.

5. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above. Order records are kept for the period required by accounting law. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe.

6. Your rights

Under data protection law you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data or ask us to delete it.
  • Object to or restrict how we use your data.
  • Withdraw consent, such as unsubscribing from emails.
  • Lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

7. Changes to this statement

We may update this statement from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page. See also our Terms & Conditions.

8. Contact us

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email support@rainydays.com or call +47 00 00 00 00. We reply within 1 to 2 business days.
